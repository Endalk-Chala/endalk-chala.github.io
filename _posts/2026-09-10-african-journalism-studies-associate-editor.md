## Associate Editor, *African Journalism Studies*

*African Journalism Studies* is seeking an **Associate Editor** to join its editorial team.

This opportunity is aimed at established scholars with a strong research and publication record in journalism and media studies, particularly those with expertise in **African and/or Global South journalism**.

### Role

The Associate Editor will contribute to:

- manuscript handling and peer review
- engagement with the editorial board and wider scholarly community
- strengthening the journal's visibility and intellectual reach

Relevant editorial, peer-review, or journal-management experience is an advantage.

### Application

Applicants should submit:

- a short CV
- a one-page statement of interest outlining research expertise and editorial experience

Send application materials to **Hayes Mabweazara, Editor** at [hayes.mabweazara@glasgow.ac.uk](mailto:hayes.mabweazara@glasgow.ac.uk).

**Deadline:** September 30, 2026

This may be especially relevant to scholars in journalism, media studies, African media, and Global South communication research.
