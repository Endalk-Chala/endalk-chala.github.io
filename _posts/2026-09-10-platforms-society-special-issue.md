## SMSociety 2026 Special Issue in *Platforms and Society*

**Journal:** *Platforms and Society* (SAGE)  
**Full-paper deadline:** December 1, 2026  
**Expected publication:** Late Summer/Early Autumn 2027

This special issue showcases research from the 2026 Social Media & Society conference and focuses on platforms, platformisation, and their social, cultural, political, and institutional consequences.

### Topics include

- algorithmic platform experience
- computational, digital, and data methods
- creators and influencers
- misinformation and disinformation
- platform cultures
- platform governance and regulation
- platformisation and datafication
- AI and large language models
- politics, policy, and regulation
- privacy, security, and trust
- social media cultures and everyday life

### Eligibility

The special issue is open to **2026 SMSociety presenters**, including presenters of papers, panels, workshops, and posters.

### Submission notes

Manuscripts may be up to 10,000 words, excluding the abstract. The lead author or a co-author must also complete peer reviews for three other submissions as part of the special-issue process.
