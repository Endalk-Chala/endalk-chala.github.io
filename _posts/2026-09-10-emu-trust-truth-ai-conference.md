## 8th International Communication and Media Studies Conference

**Organizer:** Center of Research and Communication for Peace, Eastern Mediterranean University  
**Conference dates:** November 12–14, 2026  
**Location:** Famagusta, Northern Cyprus  
**Submission deadline:** September 28, 2026

The conference theme is **“Trust and Truth in the Age of AI.”** It invites research on how artificial intelligence is reshaping communication processes, journalism, political communication, strategic communication, public trust, platforms, and media institutions.

### Topics include

- AI, verification, and fact-checking cultures
- misinformation, disinformation, and information disorder
- local journalism, conflict reporting, and public trust
- AI and political communication
- computational propaganda and persuasive communication
- platform cultures and communicative inequalities
- datafication, surveillance, and digital communication
- AI in public relations and strategic communication
- institutional trust, transparency, and organizational credibility
- crisis communication in AI-shaped media environments

The call welcomes completed research and research in progress. Proposals should explain the study’s purpose, relevance, methods, and actual or anticipated findings.
