## Special Issue: *In Media We Trust*

**Journal:** *Platform: Journal of Media and Communication*  
**Abstract deadline:** September 15, 2026, 23:59 AEST  
**Full paper deadline for invited authors:** February 1, 2027

This special issue examines trust, credibility, media literacy, contested knowledge, AI-generated content, platform infrastructures, and changing relationships between media institutions, communities, and audiences.

### Topics include

- media trust, credibility, scepticism, and distrust
- media, news, information, digital, and AI literacy
- misinformation, disinformation, rumours, and contested knowledge
- AI-generated and synthetic media
- independent, alternative, activist, and community media
- social media, creator cultures, and influencer economies
- platform governance and algorithmic visibility
- decolonial, Indigenous, and Global South perspectives on media trust
- institutional trust, public knowledge, resilience, and repair

### Eligibility

The call is intended for **current graduate students and early-career researchers within five years of completing the PhD**.

### Submission

Abstracts should be no more than **350 words** and accompanied by a brief biography for each author. Selected authors will be invited to submit a full paper.
