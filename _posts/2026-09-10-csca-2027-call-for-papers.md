## Central States Communication Association 2027 Annual Convention

**Conference dates:** April 7–11, 2027  
**Location:** Madison, Wisconsin  
**Submission deadline:** October 9, 2026, 11:59 p.m. CDT

The 2027 CSCA convention theme is **“Navigate: Finding Inspiration in Uncharted Terrain.”** Multiple interest groups are accepting submissions, including areas closely aligned with communication research, pedagogy, political communication, strategic communication, intercultural communication, social justice, and public-facing scholarship.

### Relevant interest groups include

- **Intercultural Communication** — competitive papers, panels, roundtables, workshops, and innovative programming
- **Political Communication** — competitive papers, extended abstracts, and thematic panels
- **Strategic and Applied Communication** — public relations, organizational, crisis, political, health, environmental, and risk communication
- **Basic Course** — foundational communication pedagogy, administration, and research
- **Activism, Communication Ethics, and Social Justice** — papers, panels, and performance sessions

Faculty, students, independent scholars, professionals, and community members are welcome across many of the calls. Membership is not required to create a submission profile.
