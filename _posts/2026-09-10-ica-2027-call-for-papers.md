## International Communication Association 2027 Annual Conference

The **77th Annual International Communication Association (ICA) Conference** will be held **June 3–7, 2027** in **Glasgow, Scotland**.

The 2027 conference theme is **“Centering the Human: Communication in an Age of Uncertainty.”** The theme invites scholarship on communication amid artificial intelligence, democratic backsliding, geopolitical instability, climate crisis, and other forms of uncertainty.

### Submission deadline

**November 2, 2026 at 12:00 noon Eastern Time**

The submission system opened September 1, 2026.

### Submission formats

Depending on the ICA division or interest group, submissions may include:

- full papers
- extended abstracts
- panel or roundtable proposals

The conference theme accepts full papers and panel/roundtable proposals. Division-specific requirements vary, so authors should review the call for their target division before submitting.

### Areas especially relevant to this site

ICA divisions and interest groups currently accepting submissions include:

- Communication and Technology
- Communication Law and Policy
- Intercultural Communication
- Mass Communication
- Public Relations
- Political Communication
- Mobile Communication
- Visual Communication Studies
- Public Diplomacy

### Conference information

**Dates:** June 3–7, 2027  
**Location:** Glasgow, Scotland, United Kingdom  
**Deadline:** November 2, 2026, 12:00 noon Eastern
