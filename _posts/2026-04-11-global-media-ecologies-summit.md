## Global Media Ecologies Summit 2026: Convergence and Contestation

**Dates:** October 18-21, 2026
**Location:** Singapore International Convention Centre, Singapore
**Abstract Submission Deadline:** July 15, 2026
**Early Registration Deadline:** August 30, 2026

The Global Media Ecologies Summit brings together scholars, practitioners, and policymakers to examine how media systems globally reshape cultural narratives, governance, and social movements.

### Conference Theme

"Convergence and Contestation: Power, Platforms, and Publics in Global Media" explores how local and transnational media operate amid rapid technological change, regulatory fragmentation, and competing visions of digital futures.

### Research Tracks

- **Platform Politics & Digital Governance:** Content moderation, data sovereignty, regulatory approaches across regions
- **Cultural Flows & Translation:** How media travels across borders, linguistic adaptation, and localization practices
- **Alternative & Independent Media:** Community broadcasting, activist journalism, participatory platforms
- **Journalism in Crisis:** Sustainability models, misinformation, audience trust, precarity in the profession
- **Digital Inequality:** Access, digital divides, technological colonialism, infrastructure justice
- **Oral Cultures & Digital Media:** Vernacular media practices, language preservation, oral archives
- **Audience Studies:** Reception, fan communities, participatory culture, digital publics

### Submission Details

- **Format:** Individual papers (20-minute presentations) or panel proposals (3-4 papers organized around a theme)
- **Abstract Length:** 250-300 words for individual papers; 400-500 words for panels
- **Submission Platform:** gmes2026.conferenceabstracts.org
- **Presentation Language:** English (simultaneous Mandarin and French translation available)

### Registration & Accommodations

Early bird registration (through August 30): $350 USD (students $175 USD)
Hotel partnerships available at conference rates; see website for discounted room blocks at nearby hotels.

### Travel Support

Limited travel bursaries available for scholars from Global South institutions. Apply via submission form; selection announced in August.
