My journalism and public writing spans press freedom, political communication, internet shutdowns, disinformation, platform governance, content moderation, and digital harms in Ethiopia and the Horn of Africa. I have contributed to Global Voices since 2011 and have also published in *The Guardian* through the Guardian Africa Network.
